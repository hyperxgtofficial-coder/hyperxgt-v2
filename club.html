HyperXGT — Building India’s RC Hobby Culture

More Than RC. A Community. A Culture. A Movement.

At HyperXGT, our vision goes beyond selling hobby-grade RC cars. Around the world, RC is a passionate culture built around clubs, race tracks, competitions, technical workshops, meetups, and communities. Our mission is to help build that same strong hobby-grade RC culture in India — where beginners discover the hobby, drivers connect, racers compete, and the next generation learns, builds, and grows together.

Our Core Mission

Inspiring the Next Generation of Engineers

Behind every hobby-grade RC vehicle are real engineering concepts: mechanics, electronics, radio communication, battery chemistry, suspension geometry, aerodynamics, and driving skill. We want to introduce more people to hobby-grade RC as a genuine technical interest, competitive sport, and community activity.
