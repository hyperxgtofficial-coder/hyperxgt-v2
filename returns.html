Returns & Replacement Policy

Clear, transparent guidelines for product replacements, transit damage claims, and customer satisfaction.

⚡ 7-Day Replacement Guarantee for Manufacturing Defects & Transit Damage

At HyperXGT, all high-performance RC models undergo strict pre-dispatch quality checks. If your package arrives damaged or encounters a technical manufacturing defect upon delivery, we will replace it free of charge.

1. Eligibility Criteria for Replacement

To qualify for a replacement under our 7-Day Policy, the following requirements must be met:

  • The replacement request must be registered within 7 days of delivery.
  • The product must be in its original condition, including all original packaging, manuals, remote transmitter, batteries, and included accessories.
  • An mandatory Unboxing Video showing the unopened parcel seal and initial power testing must be provided for transit damage or missing component claims.

2. Non-Eligible Conditions & Exclusions

Replacements will not be granted under the following circumstances:

  • Accidental crash damage, physical impact, water submersion beyond IP rating, or improper operation.
  • Modification of internal ESC wiring, motor soldering, or third-party battery voltage overcharging.
  • Normal wear and tear on tires, drive shafts, spur gears, and chassis bottom scratches.

3. How to File a Return / Replacement Request

Follow these quick steps to submit your claim:

  1. Open our Support Portal or email contact@hyperxgt.com with your Order Number (e.g., HX-10482).
  2. Attach photos of the outer shipping box, product, and link to your unboxing video.
  3. Our technical support team will evaluate your ticket within 24 hours and issue a reverse courier pickup label via Shiprocket Express.

4. Refund & Cancellation Terms

If an accepted order is cancelled prior to courier dispatch, a 100% full refund is issued immediately. For approved returns where exact replacement stock is unavailable, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days directly to your original payment method (UPI / Credit Card / Bank Account).
